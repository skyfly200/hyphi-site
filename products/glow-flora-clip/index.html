Glow Flora Clip

A hand-folded, hand-soldered LED bloom that clips onto whatever you bring into the night.

Each Clip is built by hand in Boulder: a folded paper flower lit from inside by a warm LED filament, on a flexible stem with a clip at the base. No app, no firmware, no fuss. Flip the switch and it glows.

Features

  • Warm LED filament glow inside a folded paper bloom
  • Runs on a single CR2032 coin cell
  • On/off switch built into the battery holder
  • Clips onto bags, costumes, tents, and gear
  • Simple, repairable, no-firmware construction

Glow Flora Home

A bouquet of programmable LED blooms, potted and ready for your shelf.

Glow Flora Home brings the blooms indoors: a potted arrangement of addressable LED flowers driven by an ESP32 running WLED, so every petal can fade, pulse, and react. Control it over Wi-Fi or from the Hyphi Hub app over Bluetooth.

Features

  • Multiple addressable LED blooms on sculpted stems
  • ESP32 brain running WLED out of the box
  • Wi-Fi control, plus Bluetooth via the Hyphi Hub app
  • Rechargeable 18650 battery, no outlet required
  • Open hardware and firmware, documented on the wiki

Smart Sprout

Living light, rooted in the wild. A sculpture that notices you.

Smart Sprout is an interactive light sculpture that responds to touch, sound, and presence. It connects to the Hyphi Hub app over Bluetooth so you can shape how it reacts.

Features

  • Responds to touch, sound, and presence
  • Bluetooth control from the Hyphi Hub app
  • Open hardware and firmware
